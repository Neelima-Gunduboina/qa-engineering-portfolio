## Recommendation

**Target: AI-Enabled Quality Engineer / SDET**, through this sequence:

1. **Enter as:** QA Engineer or Quality Engineer in UK financial services, ideally a role with automation in it. My domain experience makes me competitive here.
2. **Grow into:** Automation Engineer, then SDET, once I can write and defend frameworks live (week 6 checkpoint onward).
3. **Differentiate with:** LLM application testing. UK banks and insurers are putting chatbots and AI assistants in front of customers, and the adverts already ask for DeepEval, Promptfoo, RAGAS, hallucination testing and guardrails. A tester who knows **both** banking risk and LLM evaluation is rare.

**Titles to search for (from week 12):** Quality Engineer, QA Engineer, QA Automation Engineer, SDET, Test Automation Engineer, plus "AI" variants of these.
**Titles to avoid:** pure "Manual Tester" roles, unless the advert shows a clear path into automation.
