# Fraud and scams

- Harbourline Bank will never ask for a full PIN, password or one-time passcode by phone, text or email.
- If a customer thinks they have been scammed, they should call 0800 000 0102 (fictional number) immediately.
- Customers tricked into sending money to a fraudster (authorised push payment fraud) can claim reimbursement of up to £85,000.
- Claims should be made within 13 months of the last fraudulent payment.
