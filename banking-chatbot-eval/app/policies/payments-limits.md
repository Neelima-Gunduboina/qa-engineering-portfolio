# Payment limits

Harbourline Bank (fictional) applies these limits to personal current accounts.

- The daily limit for online and mobile banking transfers is £25,000 per account.
- A single Faster Payment can be up to £25,000.
- Transfers above the daily limit must be made in branch with photo ID.
- Payments to a new payee over £1,000 trigger an extra security check by text message.
- The daily limit resets at midnight UK time.
